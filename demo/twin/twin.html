The ground Curiosity crossed during its 2025 boxwork campaign (sols 4500–4799), rebuilt from NASA data downloaded today. Click the terrain twice to plan a walk: the route avoids steep, rough and loose ground, and can detour to the strongest mineral signals CRISM sees from orbit. It reports distance, time and radiation dose, using the dose rate Curiosity's RAD detector measured here.
